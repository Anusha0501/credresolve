# Escalation Policy

## Escalation Triggers

### Level 1 Escalation (Team Lead)
- Borrower requests to speak to supervisor
- Agent unable to resolve query
- Repeated complaints on same issue
- Promise-to-pay not honored twice

### Level 2 Escalation (Manager)
- Dispute not resolved in 15 days
- Borrower threatens legal action
- Regulatory complaint received
- Settlement request beyond policy limits

### Level 3 Escalation (Senior Management)
- RBI ombudsman complaint
- Media attention or social media escalation
- High-value account (outstanding > ₹5 lakhs)
- VIP customer escalation

## Escalation Process

### Documentation Requirements
- Complete conversation history
- All previous interactions logged
- Tool call records and outcomes
- Promise-to-pay tracking
- Dispute resolution attempts

### Timeline
- Level 1: Response within 24 hours
- Level 2: Response within 48 hours
- Level 3: Response within 72 hours

### Communication Protocol
- Escalating agent to document reason
- Escalated team to acknowledge within 4 hours
- Resolution communicated to borrower
- Original agent informed of outcome

## De-escalation Strategies

### For Angry Borrowers
- Acknowledge frustration and apologize
- Listen without interrupting
- Offer immediate resolution options
- Provide callback from senior team member

### For Refusal to Pay
- Understand underlying reason
- Offer flexible payment options
- Explain consequences calmly
- Document refusal for legal process

### For Legal Threats
- Take seriously and document
- Escalate to legal team immediately
- Cease collection activity temporarily
- Follow formal legal response process
