# RBI Collection Guidelines

## Overview
These guidelines are based on RBI regulations for debt collection practices in India.

## Ethical Collection Practices

### Contact Hours
- Collection calls should be made between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM only
- No calls on Sundays and national holidays
- Maximum 3 calls per day per borrower

### Communication Standards
- Agents must identify themselves and the organization
- Must maintain polite and professional tone at all times
- No abusive, threatening, or harassing language
- No disclosure of debt information to third parties

### Privacy Protection
- Borrower information must be kept confidential
- No discussion of debt with family members or employers
- Written consent required for any data sharing

## Fair Treatment

### Right to Information
- Borrowers have the right to know the exact outstanding amount
- Breakdown of principal, interest, and charges must be provided
- Payment history must be available on request

### Dispute Resolution
- Borrowers can raise disputes within 30 days of communication
- Disputes must be acknowledged within 5 working days
- Resolution must be provided within 30 days

### Settlement Options
- One-time settlement options must be offered for eligible cases
- Restructuring options available for genuine hardship cases
- All settlement terms must be clearly documented

## Prohibited Practices

- Physical intimidation or harassment
- Threatening legal action without proper process
- Calling at workplace without permission
- Discussing debt with neighbors or relatives
- Misleading or false statements about consequences
