# Payment Dispute Policy

## Types of Disputes

### Amount Dispute
- Borrower claims amount is incorrect
- Discrepancy in principal or interest calculation
- Unrecognized charges or fees

### Payment Not Credited
- Payment made but not reflected in account
- Delay in payment processing
- Transaction failed but amount deducted

### Unauthorized Transaction
- Transaction not initiated by borrower
- Fraudulent activity suspected
- Identity theft concerns

## Dispute Resolution Process

### Step 1: Registration
- Dispute must be registered within 30 days of communication
- Borrower to provide supporting documents
- Unique dispute reference number assigned

### Step 2: Verification
- Internal investigation initiated within 2 working days
- Payment records and transaction logs reviewed
- Bank reconciliation performed if needed

### Step 3: Resolution Timeline
- Simple disputes: 5 working days
- Complex disputes: 15 working days
- External verification required: 30 working days

### Step 4: Communication
- Borrower informed of resolution via SMS and email
- Detailed explanation provided if dispute rejected
- Corrective action taken if dispute accepted

## Supporting Documents Required

### For Amount Disputes
- Loan agreement copy
- Payment receipts
- Bank statements

### For Payment Not Credited
- Transaction receipt/UTR number
- Bank statement showing debit
- Payment confirmation screenshot

### For Unauthorized Transactions
- FIR copy (if fraud suspected)
- Identity proof
- Police complaint acknowledgment

## Borrower Rights During Dispute

- No collection activity during investigation
- No negative credit reporting during dispute period
- Right to escalate to RBI ombudsman if unsatisfied
- Compensation for delayed resolution beyond timeline
