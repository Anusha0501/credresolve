# Settlement Policy

## Eligibility Criteria

### One-Time Settlement (OTS)
- Account overdue by 60-180 days
- Original principal amount above ₹25,000
- Borrower must demonstrate genuine financial hardship
- No previous settlement in last 12 months

### Settlement Percentages

| Risk Category | Settlement Percentage |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Low Risk     | 80% of outstanding   |
| Medium Risk  | 70% of outstanding   |
| High Risk    | 60% of outstanding   |

## Settlement Process

### Step 1: Assessment
- Verify borrower's financial situation
- Check payment history and risk category
- Calculate settlement amount based on policy

### Step 2: Offer
- Present settlement offer in writing
- Specify validity period (typically 30 days)
- Outline payment terms and conditions

### Step 3: Acceptance
- Borrower must accept in writing
- Payment must be made within validity period
- Account closed upon full settlement payment

### Step 4: Documentation
- Settlement agreement signed by both parties
- No-dues certificate issued
- Credit bureau updated as "settled"

## Payment Terms

### Lump Sum Payment
- Single payment within 30 days of offer
- Additional 5% discount for payment within 7 days

### Installment Plan
- Maximum 3 installments over 90 days
- First installment due within 15 days
- Post-dated checks required

## Post-Settlement

- Account marked as "settled" in credit bureau
- No further collection activity
- Re-eligibility for new loans after 12 months
