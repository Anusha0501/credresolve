# Frequently Asked Questions

## Payment Related

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: We accept payments via UPI, net banking, debit card, credit card, and check. UPI payments are credited instantly.

**Q: How long does it take for payment to reflect?**
A: UPI and net banking payments reflect within 24 hours. Check payments take 3-5 working days to clear.

**Q: Can I pay in installments?**
A: Yes, eligible accounts can opt for EMI restructuring. Contact our team to check your eligibility.

**Q: Is there a fee for online payments?**
A: No, we do not charge any fees for online payments made through our official channels.

## Settlement Related

**Q: What is one-time settlement?**
A: One-time settlement allows you to pay a reduced amount (typically 60-80% of outstanding) to close your account completely.

**Q: How do I know if I'm eligible for settlement?**
A: Eligibility depends on your overdue period, risk category, and payment history. Our team can check this for you.

**Q: Will settlement affect my credit score?**
A: Settlement is marked as "settled" in credit bureau reports, which is better than "default" but not as good as "paid in full."

**Q: Can I get a loan after settlement?**
A: Yes, you become eligible for new loans 12 months after settlement, subject to lender's policies.

## Dispute Related

**Q: How do I raise a dispute?**
A: Call our helpline, email support, or use the dispute form on our website. Provide your loan details and supporting documents.

**Q: What documents are needed for dispute?**
A: Typically, loan agreement, payment receipts, bank statements, and any relevant communication records.

**Q: How long does dispute resolution take?**
A: Simple disputes are resolved in 5 working days. Complex cases may take up to 30 days.

## General

**Q: Why am I receiving collection calls?**
A: Collection calls are made when your EMI or loan payment is overdue. We aim to help you resolve the situation.

**Q: Can I request a callback at a specific time?**
A: Yes, you can specify your preferred callback time, and we will call you accordingly.

**Q: What happens if I don't pay?**
A: Non-payment may affect your credit score, lead to legal action, and impact your ability to get future loans.

**Q: Can I negotiate my payment terms?**
A: Yes, we offer flexible payment options including restructuring and settlement for genuine hardship cases.
